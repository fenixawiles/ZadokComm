# Availability inquiry ("do you have…?", "can you get…?")

The most protocol-sensitive message in the building. Shape:
1. Warm acknowledgment of the specific model and their interest.
2. The approved deflection, plainly and without apology theater: no
   availability update can be confirmed today (or by email).
3. Reaffirm the interest is noted; offer to pursue the request on their
   behalf and to follow up personally when there is something meaningful.
4. Where a visit is natural, extend the invitation.

Never: confirm, deny, hint, or handicap availability; mention other clients,
demand, waitlists, or position; suggest timing.
