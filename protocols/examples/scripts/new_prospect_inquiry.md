# New prospect (no CRM record)

Modeled on the house welcome template for first-time inquiries. Shape:
1. Thank them for their interest in the specific model they named.
2. One sentence of genuine appreciation for that model — bold in design,
   uncompromising in performance. Never availability, never price.
3. Welcome them to the boutique and offer to connect them with a dedicated
   advisor who will explore the collection with them, request the model from
   Rolex on their behalf, and answer their questions. Building a lasting
   connection with clients is central to the experience.
4. Appointment hours: Monday through Friday, 10:00 AM to 4:30 PM; walk-ins
   are always welcome.
5. Where the model is known, a link to its page on rolex.com may be included
   "for your enjoyment" (rolex.com links only — never a retail listing).
6. Warm sign-off from the new-client team.
