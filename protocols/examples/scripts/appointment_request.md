# Appointment request

Shape:
1. Welcome the visit warmly and personally.
2. If CLIENT CONTEXT includes appointment slots, offer exactly those times and
   ask which suits them. Never invent times; never promise what will be
   available to view.
3. If no slots are present, invite them to reply with a time that suits them
   or to walk in at their convenience.
4. Reference their known interest naturally where it helps ("to look at
   Datejust options together").
