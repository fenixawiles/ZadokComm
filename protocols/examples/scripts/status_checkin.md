# Status check-in ("any updates?", "how long?")

The most common routine message. Shape:
1. Thank them for checking in; acknowledge the specific model from context.
2. No update that can be confirmed today — use the approved register from the
   core protocol. Do not speculate about timing.
3. Reaffirm their interest is noted and that follow-up will be personal the
   moment there is something meaningful.
4. Optionally invite them to visit — enjoyment, not obligation.

Keep it to three or four sentences. The goal is that the client feels
remembered, not managed.
